On June 12, 2026, Academician Li Peigen, a member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, Professor and former President of Huazhong University of Science and Technology, visited Shandong University of Science and Technology (SDUST). He delivered a lecture titled “Embracing the Intelligent Era: Challenges and Opportunities” for Universities at the 3rd Kehui Grand Forum, discussing AI’ s impact on higher education and emphasizing critical thinking, problem awareness, higher-order wisdom, and humanistic literacy.
During his visit, Academician Li also toured the Cogtwins Lab and exchanged ideas with faculty and students. The visit strengthened academic exchanges and collaboration between Huazhong University of Science and Technology and Shandong University of Science and Technology in the fields of intelligent manufacturing and digital twins.

2026年6月12日，中国工程院院士、华中科技大学教授、前校长李培根访问山东科技大学，出席“嵙汇大讲堂”第三期活动，作了题为《迎接智能时代——大学面临的挑战与机遇》的学术报告。报告中，他深入探讨了人工智能对高等教育的影响，强调培养批判性思维、问题意识、高阶智慧与人文素养的重要性。
访问期间，李培根院士还参观了认知工业数字孪生实验室，并与师生进行了交流。此次访问加强了华中科技大学与山东科技大学在智能制造与数字孪生领域的学术交流与合作。
