Recently, Siyuan Sun, a graduate student from Cogtwins Lab, was awarded the SDUST Study Abroad Scholarship and went to Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU) in Qatar for an academic visit to Professor Samir's research group. Both sides hope that this exchange will enhance mutual understanding and lay a foundation for further in-depth educational and scientific research cooperation between the two universities.
近日，Cogtwins实验室研究生Siyuan Sun荣获SDUST留学奖学金赴卡塔尔HBKU哈马德·本·哈利法大学Samir教授课题组开展学术访问。双方希望通过此次交流，增进相互了解，为后续两校深入教育科研学术合作奠定基础。
